[On-screen: "03 — REALISTIC HUMAN MOVEMENT"]

[Gesture to show height difference.]

Their legs might not line up with the seat.

So Rockstar developed technology called runtime animation retargeting.

Basically, it helps adapt animations to different characters while the game is running.

And when you multiply that across a huge population of characters…

You start to understand how much engineering goes into making a city feel natural.

[Pause, smile.]
