# REA: edit plan (premium dark-tech style)

Based on the corrected A-roll **v3 (10:37)**. The second time column gives the matching time in the **original 13:39 rough cut (v1)**.
Style: charcoal/near-black, white text, lime `#B6FF2E` highlight, Inter, dark rounded cards. A face card sits bottom-left whenever a full-screen graphic carries the information. The face is never covered, there is no split-screen and he is never slid to one side.
Rules kept: every claim comes from your words, REA's README or the Wekan repo. Nothing is invented. Recordings you have not made are in `REA/MISSING-ASSETS.md`; until then those beats show a generated card plus the face card.

## Pacing and ratio

- A visual change every 2 to 4 s. In the preview the longest unchanged shot is 7 s, with punch-ins inside it.
- Talking head full frame (with overlays): **42%** of the runtime. Diagrams, proof and recordings: **58%**. The 65% side counts the beats that wait for your recordings.
- Punch-ins: 10 to 15% digital, on important lines, back to 100% on the next beat.
- Audio: voice cleaned by Descript Studio Sound and tone-matched across clips; minimal original electronic bed 20 dB under the voice; SFX at least 0.9 s apart (whoosh, click, ping, pop); master -14 LUFS, -1.5 dBTP.
