# Free Claude Code: 3 Shorts storyboards

Three vertical Shorts (1080×1920, 43–48 s), each driving to the long video.
- **Rough cuts:** `renders/free-claude-code-short-{A,B,C}-roughcut.mp4`.
- **Builder:** `edits/free-claude-code.shorts-build.py` (same beat lists as the tables below).

## Shared layout and retention rules
- **Frame:**
  - top 40% = Kalyan's face cam (the rough cut shows the line to record);
  - bottom 60% = screen recording, cropped and zoomed on the exact thing he's talking about;
  - one coral stamp per beat on the key word.
- **First frame = the most surprising thing.** No intro, no channel name, no "hey guys".
- **A visual change every 2–5 s** (new crop, stamp or cut). Never hold a static frame longer than 5 s.
- **Captions:** ≤ 5 words per line, centred just above the stamp zone, key word in coral `#E8775F`. Hide them during big stamps.
- **Audio:** voice ~−14 LUFS. Music bed very low (~0.08), with a drop on the reveal. SFX ≥ 0.9 s apart.
- **Ending:** the last line points to the full video. Set **Related video** in YouTube Studio so the link sits under the Short.
- **Truth:** every claim matches `edits/free-claude-code.truth-audit.md`. Blur keys, emails and work bookmarks.
- **Recording face cam:** phone vertical, eyes in the top third, a bit closer than the long video, +20% energy, smile on the hook.
