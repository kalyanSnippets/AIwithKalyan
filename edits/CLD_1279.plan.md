2. **False starts and stumbles** (`mag--`, `FF-FFMG--`, `impru-`, `fi--`, `m-`, `con--`): cut out.
   Where the clean words are split across two takes, splice them.
3. **Filler words:** cut. Scribe found only one clear filler ("Uh," at 06:34). The rest of the
   clutter is restarts and doubled words ("and, and", "understand, understand", "step by step by step").
4. **Silence:** any meaningless gap longer than 0.5 s inside a sentence is tightened to 0.3 s
   (55 pauses, saving 44 s). Joins between passages get 0.27 s. All dead air between takes is removed
   (start: 13.5 s; 05:27–05:36; 06:04–06:33; 07:09–07:27; 15:34–15:50).
5. **Natural pauses kept:**
   - 0.7 s before "…shape the edit."
   - 0.5 s after "one important rule"
   - 0.7 s after "So did Claude Code edit the entire video?"
   - 0.6 s before "…where this gets really interesting."
6. **Short holds** after three lines where you clearly meant to show something:
