## The idea

Kalyan starts a normal Reel and realises he's **inside the viewer's phone**. The viewer's thumb, the
scroll, a double-tap, low battery and a dropped phone all hit his room like natural disasters. The
whole story is built around the one thing every Instagram viewer is doing at that moment: holding
a phone with their thumb over the screen. That's what makes it Instagram-native and hard to scroll
past. The first threat *is* the scroll.

Hook: "Don't scroll! DON'T SCROLL!" A viewer about to swipe hears a person begging them not to.

Cover text: **I'M STUCK IN YOUR PHONE 📱**

## Part 1: The story (0:00–0:50). Six scenes, about 8 s each (fits one Higgsfield generation per scene)

### SCENE 1: THE THUMB (0:00–0:08)
*Locked medium shot. Normal studio.*

> **KALYAN:** Okay, today I'm going to show you something really—

A giant thumb presses on the glass **from the viewer's side**. The whole room bends inward like a
trampoline and the fairy lights swing.

> **KALYAN:** WHOA! Who's touching the screen?!

**Act it:** start the line, then flinch back hard as if the wall in front of you was punched. Hold the shocked look up at the lens.

### SCENE 2: DON'T SCROLL (0:08–0:16)
The room starts sliding **upward** out of frame, because someone is scrolling. The bottom of the next
Reel (a dancing cat) rises from below. Kalyan grabs the bottom edge of the frame with both hands
and hangs on.

> **KALYAN:** No, no, no — DON'T SCROLL! DON'T SCROLL!

The room snaps back down into place. He's breathing hard.

> **KALYAN:** …Thank you.

**Act it:** lean forward and grip something low, as if the floor is sliding up. Pull yourself back, then slump with relief.

### SCENE 3: HELLO? (0:16–0:23)
Kalyan leans close and knocks on the inside of the screen. The glass ripples like water where his
knuckles touch.

> **KALYAN:** Hello? Can you hear me? …I think I'm inside your phone.

**Act it:** lean right into the lens and knock on the air in front of it. Speak quietly and seriously.

### SCENE 4: THE DOUBLE-TAP (0:23–0:31)
A big glossy red heart bursts in the air beside him (a double-tap). He lights up.

> **KALYAN:** Oh! Thank you! Okay, okay — do that again!

Hearts start raining. Hundreds of them fill the room up to his chest, and he swims through them,
laughing.

> **KALYAN:** Okay, OKAY, that's enough love!

**Act it:** big smile, point at the heart, then wave your arms as if swimming in a ball pit. Keep your face clear.

### SCENE 5: 2% BATTERY (0:31–0:40)
The hearts vanish. The lights dim, everything turns slightly red, and the walls flicker like a
dying screen. A low warning hum.

> **KALYAN:** Wait… why is it getting dark? …Bro, your battery is at TWO percent?!
> **KALYAN:** Charge your phone! CHARGE IT!

**Act it:** look around as the light fades, then panic at the lens.

### SCENE 6: THE DROP (0:40–0:50)
The whole room lurches as the phone falls. Kalyan and the chair tilt and he grips the armrests. Then
**CRACK**: the screen in front of him splinters into a spiderweb of glass hanging in the air. Silence.

> **KALYAN:** (slowly) …Did you just drop me?

The screen goes black. A single beat of silence.

> **KALYAN (in darkness, whisper):** …Hello?

**Act it:** brace in the chair as if on a falling ride. Freeze, then deliver the line deadpan straight to the lens.
