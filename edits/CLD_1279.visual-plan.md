# CLD_1279 visual plan (step 4: design)

**Status:** awaiting approval. Only the intro sample cards are built
(`compositions/CLD_1279-intro`).

Times are on the clean rough cut (8:58.8). Three inserts with their own audio add about 57 s,
for **≈ 9:56 total**.

## Look

- **Style:** clean, premium, modern, "Apple liquid glass". Dark frosted-glass panels, thin
  white edges, soft shadows, Inter type, and Claude coral `#E8775F` as the single accent, matching
  the style of the Claude-edited sample.
- **Motion:** quick, eased slide/scale-ins (0.4–0.6 s), nothing bouncy, no random animation.
  Every graphic must explain the line being spoken.
- **Hard rules:**
  - Never cover Kalyan's face.
  - When the information matters more than his face, he moves into a **rounded card,
    bottom-left**.
  - Graphics stay off the lavender wall behind his head.
- **Jump cuts:** alternate 100% / ~112% punch-ins on cut points where he stays full-frame.

## Layout modes

| Mode | What's on screen |
|---|---|
| **K** | Kalyan full-frame (punch-in on jump cuts) |
| **K+G** | Kalyan full-frame + a glass graphic or card beside him, in the space he isn't using |
| **PiP** | Screen recording or graphic full-frame; Kalyan in the rounded card, bottom-left |
| **G** | Full-frame typography or diagram (short beats only) |
| **B** | B-roll / transition |
