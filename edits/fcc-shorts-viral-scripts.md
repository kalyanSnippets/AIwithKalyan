# Free Claude Code: 3 viral Shorts scripts (fresh voiceover)

Each script is about 35–45 s (~100–115 words) and has its own hook, subscribe line, comment line and ending.
Every short has two ending versions:
- **A · "coming soon"**: post before the long video is live.
- **B · "out now"**: post after the long video is live.

All claims match what the main video shows. The scripts never say "unlimited" or "forever", because free tiers have limits.

---

Claude Code is like a phone. The AI model inside it is the SIM card, and the SIM decides who you pay.

But this phone takes other SIMs… and some of them are completely free.

"If saving twenty dollars a month sounds good, subscribe. I test these AI tools so you don't have to."

**ENDING:**

- **B · out now:** "The full video is on my channel. Go check it out."

## Recording tips
- One file per script, ending A and ending B both recorded (I keep whichever fits the posting date).
- 2 seconds of silence at the start, 1-second pause between sections, re-say any line you stumble on.
- Punchy pace, smile on the hook. Same mic and room as the main video.

## Posting order (suggested)
1. **Script 1** (myth-buster, widest audience) with ending **A**, 2–3 days before the long video.
2. **Script 2** (offline challenge) with ending **B**, on the long video's launch day.
3. **Script 3** (proof) with ending **B**, 2–3 days after launch, to keep traffic flowing to the full video.
