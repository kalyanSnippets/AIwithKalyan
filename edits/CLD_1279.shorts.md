4. "editing video = editing a document".
5. `edit-plan.md` with CUT / SHORTEN / KEEP.
6. Sticky note: "Rule #1: Don't remove every pause."

8. Waveform: dead air cut, natural pause kept.
9. 50 s → 41 s.

11. Paper end card.

## Rebuild

```bash
python3 scripts/shorts/build_cam.py            # vertical camera tracks from the 4K originals
node scripts/shorts/build-0{1,2,3}.mjs          # HyperFrames compositions → compositions/short-0N
(cd compositions/short-0N && npx hyperframes render -o ../../renders/short-0N-master.mp4 -f 25)
bash scripts/shorts/finish.sh                   # loudness, final export, review copy, .srt
```
