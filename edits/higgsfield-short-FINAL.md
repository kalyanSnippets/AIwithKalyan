## PART C: MCP EXPLAINED IN DETAIL (for the description, pinned comment and DM replies)

### What is MCP?
MCP (Model Context Protocol) is like a **plug for AI**. It lets Claude connect to other apps and
use them for you. With the Higgsfield MCP connector, you type an idea in Claude ("make a video of
me sitting in a jungle"), and Claude sends it to Higgsfield's image and video models and brings
the result back into the chat. You don't need an API key or any code.

### What you need
- A **Higgsfield account** (free to create).
- A **Claude account** on claude.ai or the Claude desktop app. Connectors work in Claude chat and Cowork. The free trial reportedly does **not** apply in Claude Code.
- A card may be needed to start the trial, depending on your account. Check the offer screen.

### Step by step
1. Go to **higgsfield.ai/mcp** and sign in (or sign up).
2. Open **Claude** (web or desktop) and go to **Settings / Customize → Connectors → Add custom connector**.
3. Name it **Higgsfield** and paste this URL: **https://mcp.higgsfield.ai/mcp** (or just click "Add Higgsfield to Claude" on the Higgsfield page).
4. When Claude asks, **sign in to Higgsfield** to connect your account.
5. **Accept the 3-day free trial** when it's offered on your account.
6. Start a new chat and describe what you want: an image, a video, or upload your own clip and ask for an effect.

### What's free during the 3 days
- For **72 hours**, you can generate on Higgsfield's **top video and image models** for free inside Claude.
- "Unlimited" has limits. Reported caps include Seedance video at up to 720p and 15 s per clip, and images up to 2K. Some models still use credits.
- Higgsfield calls it a limited offer, with no fixed end date, so it may close any time.

### How to cancel (important)
- After 3 days, the trial **turns into a paid plan automatically** (reported as Ultra at 40% off the first month).
- To stay free, cancel before 72 hours:
  - type **"cancel auto-renewal"** in the Claude chat with Higgsfield connected, **or**
  - go to **higgsfield.ai → Manage Account → Subscription → Cancel auto-renewal**.
- Cancelling doesn't cut your trial short; you keep the remaining days.

### Tips
- Use your own footage as the reference, so your face and room stay the same.
- One effect per clip works better than five at once.
- Keep each clip short (5–15 s), then join them in an editor.
