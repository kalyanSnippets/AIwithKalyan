# Free Claude Code Shorts: recording scripts

Four vertical shorts (9:16, 30–45 s each). Each one mixes:

- **🎙 NEW VOICEOVER:** lines you record fresh (audio only). On screen while it plays: the screen recording, graphic or receipt that
  matches the line.
- **🎬 ORIGINAL CLIP:** a moment from the main video, used with its own picture *and* sound. You don't record these.

Read the 🎙 lines in order. The 🎬 lines are only there so you know what plays between them.

---

## How to record (5 minutes of setup)

1. **One file per short:** `short1.m4a`, `short2.m4a`, `short3.m4a`, `short4.m4a` (phone voice memo or WAV/M4A is fine).
2. Same mic and room as the main video if you can (it keeps your voice matching the original clips). Quiet room, phone 15–20 cm
   from your mouth.
3. **Wait 2 seconds** of silence at the start (I use it to clean background noise).
4. Read each 🎙 line, **pause 1 second**, then the next. Fluffed a line? Just say it again; I keep the best take
   and cut the mistakes, the same as the main video.
5. Energy: a little faster and punchier than the long video (Shorts viewers decide in 1–2 seconds). Smile on the hook.
6. Send the four files here in the chat (or drop them in the Drive folder "Claude video editor").

## What I do when your audio arrives
1. Transcribe it with ElevenLabs Scribe (word-level timing), keep the best take of every line, and cut stumbles and long pauses.
2. Match its loudness and tone to the original clips so the switches between new and original audio aren't noticeable.
3. Build each short in 9:16: screens/graphics on top, your face from the original camera footage at the bottom during the
   🎬 clips (and on lines marked "your face"), word-by-word captions, hook text, soft music (~20 dB under your voice), $0 cost
   for SFX.
4. Send you review copies (under 30 MB each) to mark up, then the final 1080×1920 files.

> Some lines are close to what you said in the long video. That's fine: the new recordings will be tighter and faster for
> Shorts, and the 🎬 moments keep the authentic "I'm showing you live" feel.
