# Free Claude Code: 4 Shorts plan (for approval before editing)

Source: the approved final cut (`renders/free-claude-code-3-ways-FINAL-1080p.mp4`), rebuilt from the raw 1080p camera takes,
screen recordings and HyperFrames graphics. Timestamps below are positions in the final cut.

## Shared format
- **9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps**, 30–45 s, YouTube Shorts / Reels / TikTok.
- **Layout (no upscaling):**
  - top ~55%: the screen, receipt or graphic for the current line;
  - bottom ~45%: Kalyan's face, cropped from the 1080p camera at native resolution;
  - hook beats and payoff beats go full-face for impact.
- **Captions:** word-by-word, 2–4 words at a time, Inter Black, white with the key word in coral, sitting on the seam between
  screen and face (never over the face).
- **Hook text (0–2 s):** big top-of-frame line, gone by 2.5 s.
- **Audio:**
  - same lo-fi bed family as the main video, ~20 dB under the voice;
  - soft pops on proof moments;
  - one whoosh at the hook-to-body change;
  - −14 LUFS.
- **End (last ~1.5 s):** "Full video on AI with Kalyan →" card; on YouTube it links via the related-video feature.
- **Privacy:** the same blurs as the main video (keys, email, work paths); only blurred/cropped receipts are shown.
