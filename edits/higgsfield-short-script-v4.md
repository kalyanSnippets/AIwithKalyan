## 2. Full script: start to end

### 0:00–0:04 · BEAT 1: THE SNAP (hook)
- **Frame:** normal studio. You're seated, looking straight at the lens, relaxed. The video starts on the snap; there's no intro.
- **Action:** SNAP at chest height.
- **Transition (SNAP FLASH):** a quick white-gold light pulse bursts from your fingers. In 6–8 frames the office chair ripples into a **golden throne** with red velvet.
- **You say (calm, like it's normal):** "Higgsfield just made AI video free… for three days."
- **Emotion:** cool and confident, a tiny "watch this" smile.
- **Sound:** a crisp snap plus a deep *whoomp*, and a soft shimmer as the throne forms.

### 0:04–0:10 · BEAT 2: THE MASK
- **Action:** grip under your jaw with both hands and slowly peel upward and off, then toss it aside.
- **Transition (PEEL REVEAL + PUSH-IN):** the camera does a slow push-in toward your face during the peel. A rubber mask of your own face lifts away, and underneath is **hero you**: aviator sunglasses, styled hair, a sharp beard. The push-in settles as the mask lands off-frame.
- **You say:** "And no, I don't have a VFX studio. This is my bedroom."
- **Emotion:** dry and honest. A small shrug on "bedroom".
- **Sound:** a rubbery peel and a light *thwap* as the mask lands.

### 0:10–0:16 · BEAT 3: THE T-SHIRT
- **Action:** pull the tee up and off over your head in one move.
- **Transition (FABRIC WIPE):** the moment the shirt passes over your face is the cut point; the fabric works as a natural wipe. When your face comes out, you're already in a **sharp black suit** with an open-collar white shirt. You toss the tee aside.
- **You say:** "I just filmed myself on my phone… and Claude cut every scene for me."
- **Emotion:** a little proud, like you're sharing a secret.
- **Sound:** a cloth whoosh and a suit "cinch" snap.

### 0:16–0:21 · BEAT 4: THE JUNGLE
- **Action:** SNAP.
- **Transition (WORLD DISSOLVE):** the walls crack into floating leaves and light particles, which blow away like wind and reveal a **sunlit jungle**: giant leaves, mist, golden light rays. You and the throne stay in exactly the same place. It takes about 1 s.
- **You say:** "Then Higgsfield added all the effects. Right inside Claude."
- **Emotion:** calm. You don't even look around, and that's the joke.
- **Sound:** a snap, then a whoosh of wind and leaves as birds and insects fade in.

### 0:21–0:35 · BEAT 5: THE OFFER (in the jungle)
- **Visual:** a slow, gentle push-in over 14 s. A blue butterfly drifts past at about 0:26, and light rays shift.
- **On-screen text (keep it off your face, bottom third):** **higgsfield.ai/mcp** at 0:21–0:27, then **3 DAYS FREE** at 0:27–0:30, then **⏰ Set a reminder** at 0:30–0:35.
- **You say:**
  - "Go to higgsfield.ai slash MCP. Connect it to Claude. Accept the trial."
  - "Three days. Top video models. Free." *(count them on your fingers: 1, 2, 3)*
  - "Just one thing — after three days, it becomes a paid plan. So set a reminder." *(lean in slightly, friendly warning)*
- **Emotion:** helpful friend, not a salesman. A warm tone on "set a reminder".
- **Sound:** jungle ambience under, a soft pop on each text card.

### 0:35–0:45 · BEAT 6: BACK TO REAL (the emotional ending)
- **Action:** SNAP.
- **Transition (SMASH CUT TO SILENCE):** everything snaps back instantly to the real studio, office chair and white tee. **All sound cuts to silence** for half a second: no music, no jungle.
- **You say (softer, slower, real):**
  - "Honestly… a few years back, this needed a full VFX team."
  - "Today, it's just me, my phone… and one idea."
  - *(small genuine smile)* "Full tutorial is coming. Follow, so you don't miss it."
- **Emotion:** this is the heart of the video. Drop the act and speak from the heart, as a creator talking to creators.
- **Sound:** silence, then a soft warm piano or pad fades in under "one idea". End on a gentle note.
- **Last frame:** hold your smile for 1 s. Optional end text: **AI with Kalyan**.

## 4. How to film it

- **Setup:** one take, locked camera, the same chair, outfit and lighting as the 18 Nov takes. Shoot 9:16 if you can.
- **Snaps:** do them at chest height so your hand is clearly in frame.
- **Mask:** you don't need a real mask; mime the peel slowly and clearly.
- **T-shirt:** wear a plain vest under the tee, and pull it off in one smooth move.
- **Pacing:** pause about half a second after every gesture so each transition has room.
- **Two tones:** beats 1–5 are flat and cool. Beat 6 switches to soft and real. Practise that switch.
