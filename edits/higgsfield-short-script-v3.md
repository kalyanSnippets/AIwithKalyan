## The idea

He sits in his chair and explains the Higgsfield trial **as if nothing is happening**. Each gesture
triggers a transformation, and he never reacts. The deadpan contrast is the joke, and the explanation
is the content, so the viewer gets the offer while watching the magic.

The arc goes from creator in a T-shirt to movie hero on a throne in a jungle, then a snap back to normal.

**Cover text:** ONE SNAP. 🫰
**Hook:** the first snap happens in the first second.

## How to film it (one take, locked camera)

- **Setup:** use the same chair, outfit, lighting and framing as the 18 Nov takes, and shoot 9:16 if you can.
- **The snaps:** snap close to your face at chest height so the hand is clearly in frame.
- **The mask:** you don't need a real mask. Grip the skin under your jaw with both hands and peel upward and off in one slow, clear move, then toss it aside. Higgsfield generates the mask in your hands.
- **The shirt:** wear a plain fitted vest under the tee. Pull the tee off over your head in one move and keep talking straight away. Higgsfield replaces what's underneath with the suit.
- **Delivery:** keep the voice flat and calm throughout, with no reaction to any change. That's the joke.
- **Pacing:** pause about half a second after every gesture so each transformation has room.
