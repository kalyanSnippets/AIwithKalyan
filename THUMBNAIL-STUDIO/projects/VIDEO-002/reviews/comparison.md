## Rubric

- **Mobile legibility** (weight 20): Can the text and focal point be read at 320x180 in a feed?
- **Curiosity & clarity** (weight 15): Is there a clear question or tension a viewer wants resolved, without confusion?
- **Focal hierarchy** (weight 15): Is there one immediately identifiable focal point, then text, then supporting detail?
- **Subject isolation** (weight 10): Does the subject separate cleanly from the background (edge, contrast, light)?
- **Visual uniqueness** (weight 10): Does it stand apart from the reference thumbnails instead of copying them?
- **Title/thumbnail synergy** (weight 10): Does the image add information the title does not repeat?
- **Authenticity & accuracy** (weight 15): Is every claim, result and graphic true to the video? Real face, real evidence?
- **Brand consistency** (weight 5): Does it feel like AI with Kalyan (coral accent, dark tech, clean type) without being a clone of the others?

Automated QA caps: a headline below the mobile size minimum caps legibility at 2, small secondary text at 4, a contrast warning at 3, text over the face caps hierarchy at 2, and a missing or blank export scores 1 everywhere.
