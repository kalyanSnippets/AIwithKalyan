# VIDEO-002 research analysis: "Claude Code for $0, 3 ways"

Observed 2026-11-12 with vidIQ (55 credits). Reference images could not be viewed (i.ytimg.com blocked); visual notes come from vidIQ thumbnail feedback and titles only.

## What the niche looks like
- **Extremely crowded and very recent.** 17 relevant videos in the last 3 months, 6 of them published in the last 10 days. Titles converge on the same words: "FREE", "Forever", "Unlimited", "No Subscription", "$0".
- **Two exact-angle competitors already exist:** "3 Ways" (Outskill, 147.6K views, 15x; Bhupesh Thukral, 10.6K, 10x) and **OmniRoute** setups (Amidia "1.6 Billion Free Tokens", 1.07M views; Remigiusz Zalewski, 46.8K, 123x).
- **Biggest hits promise a concrete payoff, not a method name:** "Runs On Your Computer" (1.1M), "1.6 Billion Free Tokens" (1.07M), "Unlimited".
- The obvious visual concept (face + Claude logo + FREE/$0 + crossed-out $20) returned no strong direct matches in vidIQ's similarity search, but the title wording is saturated. So the thumbnail has to carry the differentiation: proof.

## Gaps / implications for us
1. **Proof is the gap.** Nobody's title or (per feedback) thumbnail shows a *receipt*. We have real ones: the OpenRouter activity page ($0.00, 3 requests), and the OmniRoute log row "claude-sonnet-4.5 · Kiro · 200". "Real Claude Sonnet, $0, with receipts" is ours.
2. **Clean structure wins** (99 vs 26–51): one focal point, aligned blocks, red/coral accent, no clutter.
3. **Visual guidance**: a single arrow/circle on the proof element is flagged as missing on 4 of 6 references.
4. **Natural expression**: exaggerated/over-processed faces were penalised twice. Use Kalyan's real, natural frames.
5. **"3 ways" as structure**: a clean 3-panel (offline / cloud / combo) reads at a glance; Outskill's clean 3-ways layout scored 99, so don't copy its layout; make ours about the $0 result.
6. Avoid "Unlimited"/"Forever": the video is honest that nothing is unlimited.
