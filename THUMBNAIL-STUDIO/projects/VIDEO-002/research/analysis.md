# VIDEO-002 research analysis (2026-10-31, vidIQ, 35 credits)

## What the niche looks like
- REA exploded in the last 3 weeks (published 6–22 Oct). Big channels covered it already (Low Level 3.5M views, Nick Saraev
  1.7M, AI Search 1.1M), so "what is REA" is crowded. Small channels still break out: Eric Tech 41x, MG 44x, Azou 45x
  (2.8K subs), AI DOCTOR SURESH 39x (1.5K subs). The topic is still pulling viewers to small channels.
- Titles that broke out promise a **concrete result** ("Clone Any Software In Minutes", "I Cloned REA's Own Website") or a
  **test** ("I Tested The Viral…"). Generic hype titles ("Just Changed AI Forever!", 2.2x) did worse.
- Almost nobody shows a **specific app they cloned** — most are "reverse engineer anything/any app". A real, named
  result (a Kanban board, side by side) is a gap.

## vidIQ thumbnail feedback on 4 references (scores 53–80)
- All were praised for sharpness; the differences came from composition.
- Penalties that repeated:
  - clutter / bad negative space (3 of 4)
  - exaggerated facial expression (2 of 4)
  - no clear focal point or eye guidance (2 of 4)
  - washed-out / poor lighting (2 of 4)
- Nick Saraev's thumbnail (62) was told "comparison is not immediately clear — use a clean side-by-side or split-screen".
  Our core idea *is* a side-by-side, so it must read instantly.
- Matches VIDEO-001 learnings: sharp, high-contrast, uncluttered, natural expression.

## Saturation check
- Similar-thumbnail search for "two near-identical app screenshots side by side + face + which one is real" returned
  only unrelated videos. Weak evidence the side-by-side "spot the real one" look is not common here.

## Implications for our options
1. **Spot the real one (Before/After + Curiosity):** two big, clean board screenshots side by side with "?" labels;
   Kalyan small, natural. Headline ≤ 3 words ("WHICH IS REAL?"). One focal point: the pair.
2. **Face + result:** Kalyan's natural, slightly impressed expression + the REA Board frame + "1 LINK → APP". Avoid an
   exaggerated face.
3. **Hype test (Minimalist Tech):** "400K ★" stat + "WORTH IT?" on ink, with the cloned board as evidence. Answers the
   honest-review angle.
- Keep generous negative space, dark ink background, coral accent, sharp real frames.
- Don't claim "100% identical", "any app" or "in minutes" (it took ~20).
