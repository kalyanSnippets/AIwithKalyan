## Avoided wording
- "Fully automated", "without touching the timeline" or "AI replaced my editor": the video contradicts them.
- "in 10 minutes": an unverified claim from the sample short's script.
- Close copies of competitor titles, e.g. "Claude Edited My Entire CapCut Video (I Didn't Touch the
  Timeline)" and "I Fully Automated My Video Editing Using Claude Code" (see THUMBNAIL-STRATEGY.md).

## Top 3
1. **#3 "I Gave Claude Code My Raw Footage. Here's What It Made"**: the best truth and curiosity
   balance. It pairs with thumbnail 01 (RAW → EDITED).
2. **#4 "Can Claude Code Actually Edit a YouTube Video?"**: an honest question with good search
   overlap. It pairs with 05 (CAN IT EDIT?) or 02 (AI EDITOR?!).
3. **#5 "I Let Claude Code Edit My Video (Full 6-Step Workflow)"**: the safest hybrid. It pairs
   with 02 or 01.
