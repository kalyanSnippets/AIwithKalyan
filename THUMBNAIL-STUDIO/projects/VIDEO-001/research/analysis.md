## Analysis

### What the evidence shows

**Topic demand is real and recent.** In the last three months several English-language
"Claude edits my video" videos broke out relative to their channels:

- "I Fully Automated My Video Editing Using Claude Code": 507k views, breakout 40.2, 852 views/hour.
- "Claude Edited My Entire CapCut Video (I Didn't Touch the Timeline)": 507k views, about 1,000–1,235 views/hour.
- "I Had Opus 5.5 Create And Edit ENTIRE Videos": breakout 113 on a channel with 3.2k subscribers.
- "Claude + DaVinci Resolve": breakout 113 on a channel with 3.0k subscribers.

Small channels breaking out on this exact premise suggests the topic, not channel size, is
carrying the views.

**Title conventions (from titles, observed directly):**
- First-person experiment framing: "I let / I had / I fully automated…".
- The tool pairing named up front: "Claude + CapCut", "Claude + DaVinci", "Claude … Premiere Pro".
- Scope words that promise totality: "ENTIRE", "Fully", "every".
- A proof hook in brackets: "(RAW results)", "(Full Walkthrough)", "(I Didn't Touch the Timeline)".

**Thumbnail evidence (vidIQ thumbnail-score feedback on the live thumbnails; images not viewed
directly):**
- Thumbnails that scored highest (98, 90, 87, 86) were consistently described as **sharp, bright
  and high-contrast**. The 98 also had a **sharp, well-lit face** and a **clear value proposition**.
- The most common weakness, flagged on 4 of 6, was **clutter or poor negative space** and **high
  visual entropy** (about 7.1–7.4). The fix suggested each time was a tighter crop, a cleaner
  background, or one dominant subject.
- One thumbnail lost points for having **no open question or mystery** (Premiere Pro video, 65).
  Another lost points for an **exaggerated, performative expression** (Opus 5.5 motion graphics, 49).
- **Views ≠ thumbnail quality.** The Opus 5.5 motion-graphics video has 601k views and a breakout
  of 88, yet its thumbnail scored 49. Topic and title can carry a video, so nothing here is
  treated as proven CTR.

**Saturation check (vidiq_similar_thumbnails).** A text search for "a creator's face beside a video
editing timeline with an AI logo, bold short text, dark background" returned mostly weak
performers (breakout about 1–7, mostly under 10k views). The obvious layout, face + timeline + logo,
is common and undifferentiated.

**Change history.** The two closest references (5N1moUCsVhI, HzXD4GVqXwM) had no observed title
or thumbnail changes and no A/B tests, so there is no change evidence for this topic.

### Repeated conventions
1. Face on one side, tool or result on the other.
2. Named tools: CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci or Claude branding as the "what".
3. Short, loud claims of totality ("ENTIRE", "FULLY").
4. Bright, saturated colour pops (red accents were rewarded).

### Under-used opportunities for AI with Kalyan
1. **Genuine before/after evidence.** Kalyan has the real raw camera frame and the real finished
   frame from this video. Competitors mostly show a timeline or a logo, not the actual
   transformation.
2. **Clean, low-entropy composition.** vidIQ's most common criticism was clutter. One dominant
   subject on a dark, quiet background is both on-brand and a gap in this niche.
3. **An honest open question** ("who edited this?") rather than an exaggerated reaction face.
   Kalyan's footage has natural, believable expressions, and an exaggerated one was penalised.
4. **True, specific proof points** that competitors can't copy: 25/25 automated QA checks, six
   steps, the raw 16:46 recording turned into the finished video.
5. **Avoid the closest competitor's wording**: "I Didn't Touch the Timeline", "Gamechanger".

### Implications for the five concepts
- Keep at most 4 words, one focal point and a dark quiet background. Light the face/subject, and
  put coral on exactly one element.
- At least two concepts should use real evidence frames from the video, not mock-up UI.
- Use a believable expression (k121, raised brows) instead of a staged shock face.
- Don't use the Claude logo: no officially sourced logo file is in the project. The drawn coral
  starburst in Kalyan's edit is not Anthropic's mark, so "Claude Code" appears as text.
